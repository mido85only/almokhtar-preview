00 — ABOUT US

Quality excellence at every step.

AL Mokhtar is a trusted leader in the production and export of a wide range of salt types. Established over 20 years ago in Egypt, the company has consistently evolved to meet market demands and industry challenges with innovation and dedication.

Today, AL Mokhtar proudly operates large saline and rock salt quarries, supplying premium-quality sea and rock salt. All products are manufactured under strict quality standards and are certified with ISO 22000 and ISO 9001. In addition, we run two specialized factories for producing refined and food-grade salt.

At AL Mokhtar, quality is at the heart of everything we do. From staff training to customer education, we are committed to delivering superior salt products—especially in de-icing and industrial applications. Our facilities are carefully managed to ensure dry and clean storage, maintaining product integrity.
